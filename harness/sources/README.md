# Harness sources

The inputs of the harness that were entered by hand or fetched once: for poll availability (spec §5.3), the Wikipedia fieldwork tables, the house map, the fieldwork corrections and the publication dates; then the respondent-count and fundamentals corrections, the economy release dates, the house table and the coalition events. The results sources kept here (`hagstofa/`, `althingi/`, `seats_2021_official.csv`) are described in `harness/truth/README.md`. Every source is public and was retrieved on 2026-10-23, unless its section gives another date.

The tables are derived from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0) and cite it by revision. The raw page text is not committed. To rebuild, fetch the three revisions from the MediaWiki API and run the parser, which writes nothing unless its checks pass and reproduces the committed files byte for byte:

```sh
dir=$(mktemp -d)
for oldid in 1380684596 1324451223 1377749798; do
  curl -sS --fail -A 'kosningaspa-harness/0.1 (<contact email>)' -o "$dir/$oldid.json" \
    "https://en.wikipedia.org/w/api.php?action=parse&oldid=$oldid&prop=wikitext&format=json&formatversion=2"
done
LC_ALL=en_US.UTF-8 Rscript harness/sources/wikipedia/poll_fieldwork.R "$dir"
```

- **Columns.** `fieldwork` is the page's text. `start` and `end` are parsed from it. `n` is the sample-size column (`NA` for a dash, N/A or TBA). D, S, B, V, P, C, F, M and J are shares in percent as printed, blank where the page gives none (for example J throughout 2017, which has no J column, and M until late September 2017). The election-result rows are dropped, as are the response-rate, A (Björt framtíð), L, Y, Others and Lead columns.
- **`note`.** `month-only` when the page gives only a month (start and end are its first and last days). `two periods` for Maskína's "3–7 Nov & 23–26 Nov 2023" (start of the first range, end of the second).
- **Single dates.** The 2021 page gives the publication date where fieldwork is unknown, and the 2024 page does too at least once (Prósent "18 Oct 2024" is the date mbl.is published that poll), so a single date can be either.
- **2021.** The 38 rows were copied from an earlier fetch of the same page (2026-10-09). The parser keeps them as they are, with line endings normalised to LF. It looks each one up in oldid 1324451223 by house, start, end and shares (to two decimals), and stops unless the revision's row has the same fieldwork text, n and note. All 38 are there, and the revision lists no row in their range (fieldwork ending 2020-12-03 to 2021-09-25) that they lack. It would append any such row.

## Fieldwork corrections

A correction gives the date of the availability order; a later first publication still floors it (see Publication dates), as it does for five of the seven national corrections.

- **MMR 2021-07-11 (national).** Fieldwork ran from 8 to 14 July 2021, according to the poll's first publication, on [mbl.is on 16 July 2021](https://www.mbl.is/frettir/kosning/2021/07/16/stjornarmyndun_akaflega_erfid/). The row has no `lokadagur` and Wikipedia's 2021 page lists no such row, so only the `house_max` fallback dated it (2021-07-27).
- **Maskína 2021-07-19 (national).** Fieldwork ran from 13 to 26 July 2021, according to [Maskína's July 2021 report](https://maskina.is/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/St%C3%B6%C3%B02_Fylgiflokka_J%C3%BAl%C3%AD-heimas%C3%AD%C3%B0a.pdf). Its n of 847 is the row's, and its shares are the row's without the 1% Annað. Wikipedia's 2021 page dates the poll "July 2021" only, which gave the row an end of 31 July, after the poll's first publication (Vísir, 28 July 2021).
- **MMR 2021-09-02 (national).** Fieldwork ran from 31 August to 3 September 2021, according to the poll's publication on [mbl.is on 6 September 2021](https://www.mbl.is/frettir/kosning/2021/09/06/fylgi_framboda_i_jarnum/). Its D, B, S and V shares and its n of 957 are the row's. Wikipedia's 2021 page lists no such row. The poll was found because the `house_max` fallback alone kept it out at F = 2021-09-11.
- **Maskína 2021-09-03 (national).** Fieldwork ran from 31 August to 6 September 2021, according to [maskina.is on 8 September 2021](https://maskina.is/fylgi-flokka-i-september/) and the report it links. ## Publication dates

`poll_publication.csv` (level, date, fyrirtaeki, published, source, note) records the day a poll was first published. `level`, `date` and `fyrirtaeki` are the keys of the snapshot poll.

- **What it holds.** 69 national polls: every poll that a forecast date F in `TARGETS` admits with `available_from` in (F − 45, F], the 45 days before F. 68 rows were gathered on 23 Oct 2026 from the first public report of each poll: a news article (mbl.is, Vísir, RÚV, Fréttablaðið) or the firm's own page; 12 rows cite a Wayback capture of the page. The 69th, Maskína 2024-05-23, came with its fieldwork correction, which moved it into the window of F = 2024-06-03.
- **`note`.** The evidence: the page's metadata field and value (`datePublished`, `article:published_time`, `time datetime`) or a short quote, the Wayback capture where one is cited, and after ` | ` the fieldwork the source gives.
- **The floor.** No poll is available before it was published. `available_from` is the later of the date the availability order gives and `published`, and a kjördæmi poll is never available before its national twin; the floor never lowers a date. In the national rule order `published` comes after a correction, `lokadagur` and a Wikipedia match and before the fallback, so a known fieldwork end always gives at least end + 1, and `published` decides alone only where no end is known. In the kjördæmi order it comes after a correction and the national twin, so there it decides alone only for a poll with neither. Ten polls were published on their last fieldwork day; each gets end + 1, whichever source gives the end. A publication date before a poll's known fieldwork end stops the build. Maskína 2021-07-19, published on 28 July 2021, would trip it with Wikipedia's "July 2021" end (31 July), but its correction (26 July) outranks Wikipedia. Brynjólfur decided the floor on 23 Oct 2026.
- **Polls it delays past a forecast date.** Three polls that end + 1 admitted were first published after F. MMR 2021-09-09: admitted at F = 2021-09-11, first published by [mbl.is on 13 Sep 2021](https://www.mbl.is/frettir/kosning/2021/09/13/fylgi_framboda_komid_a_hreyfingu_inn_a_midju/); MMR's own 2021 pages date each poll at its fieldwork end, so a page date alone is not publication evidence. Gallup 2024-10-07: admitted at F = 2024-10-14, the first half of Gallup's October 2024 monthly poll, first reported by RÚV on 1 Nov 2024. Prósent 2024-10-10: admitted at F = 2024-10-14, first published in Prósent's chart of 25 Oct 2024.
- **Prósent 2024-09-26 (national).** Published 2024-10-18 by mbl.is: this is the known duplicate above, which stays in the data. Brynjólfur approved the row on 23 Oct 2026.

## Respondent-count corrections

`poll_n_corrections.csv` (level, date, fyrirtaeki, n_total_data, n_total_reported, source) corrects, harness-side, a poll whose respondent count in the data is wrong; `data/`, the source sheet and the snapshot stay as they are. `level`, `date` and `fyrirtaeki` are the keys of the snapshot poll. `read_inputs()` (`R/harness/info_set.R`) applies the table to the poll rows that `info_set()` hands a forecast. It computes availability and the entry table first, from the snapshot as it is, so `availability.csv` and `entry_table.csv` stay rebuilds of the snapshot.

- **The rule.** Each of the poll's rows is multiplied by `n_total_reported` / the poll's own n total in the snapshot, unrounded. The data's n are weighted, fractional counts, so nothing is rounded: the shares are unchanged and the rows sum to `n_total_reported` to floating-point precision. A correction applies only while the poll's n total, rounded to a whole number, equals `n_total_data`. Otherwise it warns and leaves the poll as it is, so once the sheet is fixed the row does nothing and can be deleted. A row naming no poll stops the build.
- **Fréttablaðið, Stöð 2 og Vísir 2017-10-10 (national).** The data's rows sum to 8 037,76, ten times the 804 respondents the poll's publication reports: "náðist í 804 samkvæmt lagskiptu úrtaki 10. október" ([Vísir, 11 October 2017](https://www.visir.is/g/2017171019823/vg-enn-langstaerst-en-piratar-tapa-fylgi-), retrieved 2026-10-23). The article's shares are the row's (Vinstri græn just under 30%, Sjálfstæðisflokkurinn just over 22%, Píratar 8,5%, Viðreisn 3,3%). Of the firm's other eleven polls in the window, nine have an n of about 800 and two of about 1 600. The guard compares 8 038, the total rounded. After the correction the rows sum to 804 and every share is as before. Brynjólfur decided on the harness-side correction on 23 Oct 2026.

## Fundamentals corrections

`fundamentals_corrections.csv` (date, flokkur, column, value_data, value_corrected, source, note) corrects, harness-side, a value of the snapshot's `fundamentals_data.csv` that breaks the rule the rest of its column follows; `data/`, the source sheet and the snapshot stay as they are. `date` and `flokkur` are the keys of the fundamentals row, as the data labels it, and `column` the value corrected. `read_inputs()` (`R/harness/info_set.R`) applies the table to the fundamentals that `info_set()` hands a forecast, through `fundamentals_inputs()`. A correction applies only while the snapshot value equals `value_data`. Otherwise it warns and leaves the value as it is. A row naming a column that is not a value column (`date` and `flokkur` are keys), or no row or more than one, stops the build.

- **Sjálfstæðisflokkurinn 2017-10-28, `incumbent_years`: 5 → 4.** The data codes incumbency by the coalition formed after the previous election, not by the cabinet in office at E: `incumbent` marks that coalition's parties, and `incumbent_years` is E's year minus the year of the election after which the party's unbroken run in such coalitions began. D in 2009 shows that "the party's current run in government" is the wrong reading: its coalition with Samfylkingin, formed after the 2007 election, fell in January 2009, yet the data codes D incumbent with 2009 − 1991 = 18, and Vinstri græn, in the cabinet that governed until that election, not incumbent. Every other incumbent row from 2003 on follows the rule: D is 3 in 2016, 8 in 2021 and 11 in 2024, Framsókn 3 in 2016 and 4 and 7 after its 2017 return, Vinstri græn 4 and 7. Viðreisn and Björt framtíð, in the cabinet formed on 11 January 2017 after the 2016 election, are coded 1, counting from that election, rather than the 0 that counting from the cabinet's formation would give. The difference is immaterial: the model enters `incumbent_years` as its log when positive and as 0 otherwise (`R/stan_data.R:382`), and log 1 = 0. The harness leaves them as they are. D entered government with Sigmundur Davíð Gunnlaugsson's cabinet, formed on 23 May 2013 with D and B ([Wikipedia, oldid 1333217382](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?oldid=1333217382): infobox `date_formed` 23 May 2013; the government's register, [Ríkisstjórnartal](https://www.stjornarradid.is/rikisstjorn/sogulegt-efni/rikisstjornartal/): "Ráðuneyti Sigmundar Davíðs Gunnlaugssonar: 23. maí 2013 - 7. apríl 2016"; both retrieved 2026-10-24). D has been in the coalition formed after each election since (see Coalition events below), so its 2017 value is 2017 − 2013 = 4. The value enters the 2017 target row and the training rows of the 2021 and 2024 forecasts. Brynjólfur decided on the correction on 24 Oct 2026.

## Economy release dates

`economy_release.csv` (election, covariate, period, value, first_release, source, note) records the day Hagstofa first published each economy covariate of a target row, for each window election `info_set()` can build: 2017-10-28, 2021-09-25 and 2024-11-30 (2016-10-29 has no previous kjördæmi results). `vnv` is the 12-month change of the CPI at July of E − 1 (`period` is that month), and `growth` is THJ01000's "Verg landsframleiðsla á mann, magnbreyting frá fyrra ári" for E − 1 (`period` is the year), as `R/prepare_economy_data.R` reads them. `value` is the snapshot's value at E. The dates were gathered on 2026-10-24; `source` holds each URL and `note` the evidence.

- **The check.** `read_economy_release()` (`R/harness/info_set.R`) stops on a missing or unparsable value, another covariate or a row listed twice. `info_set()` stops if a `value` differs from the snapshot's `economy_data.csv` at E, or if F is before a `first_release` of E, naming the covariate. It carries the rows as `economy`, and `validate_inputs()` fails "economy covariate not known at F" on an `economy` row first released after F, or a target `vnv` or `growth` that differs from its `economy` value.
- **CPI.** Each date is Hagstofa's news release on that July's CPI, which states the 12-month change: 22 Jul 2016 (1,1%), 24 Jul 2020 (3,0%) and 21 Jul 2023 (7,6%).
- **Growth.** Each date is the first annual GDP estimate for E − 1: the Hagtíðindi issue "Landsframleiðslan 2016" of 9 Mar 2017, and the news releases of 26 Feb 2021 (2020) and 29 Feb 2024 (2023). The 2016 and 2020 publications state the per-capita volume change itself (5,5% and −8,2%). The 2023 release states only GDP growth (4,1%), but THJ01000, which holds the per-capita series, was updated with 2023 the same day: a Wayback capture of the table page on 3 Mar 2024 reads "Latest update 2/29/2024", reference time 1945-2023. That table date is the row's `first_release`. THJ01000 was likewise updated on 9 Mar 2017 (capture of 22 Mar 2017: "Síðast uppfært 20170309") and held 2020 by 6 Mar 2021.
- **Every `TARGETS` cell is after both releases.** The earliest F of each election, 2017-10-14, 2021-03-29 and 2024-06-03, falls after the growth releases of 9 Mar 2017, 26 Feb 2021 and 29 Feb 2024. The earliest F any cell can have is the later of E's two dates.
- **The values are not the first-release values.** The dates bound only when a figure was first public; the values are the snapshot's later vintage, a revision leak spec §5.3 rule 5 accepts. Growth was first published as 5,5 for 2016 and −8,2 for 2020, against the snapshot's 4,7 and −7,8.

## House table

`house_table.csv` (fyrirtaeki, house, lineage) gives the house and house lineage of every `fyrirtaeki` in the snapshot's national and kjördæmi polls (spec §7 D7), entered on 2026-10-23. `Kosning`, the official results, is house `election`. Every pollster is its own house and its own lineage: the spec's §12 Q2 default, under which Prósent does not continue MMR. `read_houses()` (`R/harness/tables.R`) stops on a missing value or a `fyrirtaeki` listed twice.

## Coalition events

`coalition_events.csv` (cabinet, from, to, parties, pm, caretaker, source_url) records the cabinet in office on each day from 2016-04-07 (spec §7 D9), entered by hand on 2026-10-23. Each row runs from `from` up to the day before `to`; `to` is NA for the cabinet still in office. `parties` and `pm` are list letters. `caretaker` is TRUE once the prime minister has resigned for the cabinet and the President has asked it to stay on until a new one is formed (a *starfsstjórn*). `read_coalitions()` (`R/harness/tables.R`) stops on columns other than these, a missing or unparsable value, a list letter outside `LETTER_PARTY` and A, a `pm` not among the row's `parties`, a cabinet whose rows are not contiguous, an empty file, an open `to` anywhere but on the last row, a last row that is not open, a `to` not after its `from`, a gap or an overlap.

- **What gets a row.** A new row starts wherever the parties holding ministries or the caretaker status change, at or before the last forecast date F of a cycle. A caretaker spell after an election (the cabinet resigning after E and sitting until the next one forms) is not split out, because no F falls inside one: Sigurður Ingi Jóhannsson's cabinet resigned on 30 October 2016 and sat as a caretaker until 11 January 2017, inside its single row.
- **Against the plan.** The plan listed seven rows. The sources add three caretaker spells before a last F: the 2017 cabinet from 16 September 2017, Katrín Jakobsdóttir's second cabinet from 7 to 9 April 2024, and Bjarni Benediktsson's second cabinet with all three parties from 15 to 17 October 2024. No composition changes, so the Brier event, the incumbents and the PM at every F are the plan's.
- **Sources.** Each row's `source_url` is the page that establishes it: an English Wikipedia cabinet page at a pinned revision, a statement by the President of Iceland, or a stjornarradid.is page. The government's register of cabinets, [Ríkisstjórnartal](https://www.stjornarradid.is/rikisstjorn/sogulegt-efni/rikisstjornartal/), gives each cabinet's dates and ministers, and a minister who left early carries "til" and a date. It confirms seven of the dates: the cabinet starts and ends 2016-04-07, 2017-01-11, 2017-11-30, 2021-11-28, 2024-04-09 and 2024-12-21, and 2024-10-17, when the Vinstri græn ministers left. It does not record caretaker spells, so the three caretaker starts rest on other sources: 2017-09-16 and 2024-04-07 on the President's statements, 2024-10-15 on the stjornarradid.is notice. A Wayback Machine capture is linked beside a President's statement where one exists. All pages were retrieved on 2026-10-23. althingi.is refuses scripted requests, so it was not used.
