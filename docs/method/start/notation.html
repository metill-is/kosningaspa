The plain picture

This page collects what the other chapters define. It derives nothing: each row points to the chapter that does. It covers the chapters written so far, 1.1, 1.4 and 3.1, and each new chapter adds its own rows.

  • Indices. $p$ is a party (a list), $t$ a date on the poll-date grid, $n$ a row of data (a poll or a result), $h$ a house (a polling firm) and $e$ an epoch. In chapters 1.3 and 1.4, $k$ is a kjördæmi; in 1.4, $i$ and $j$ are lists. Section Clashes lists the letters that mean different things in different chapters.
  • Scales. "Logit scale" and "log-ratio scale" both mean the scale of the linear predictor $\eta$. In every model here $\eta$ sums to zero over the parties, so it is the centred log-ratio (clr) of the shares it gives: each party's log share minus the mean log share (chapter 1.1). "pp" means percentage points of vote share.
  • Stan names are in code type and come from the model files on master: Stan/polling_and_fundamentals_kjordaemi.stan for the forecast model, and Stan/polling_watch_v4.stan and Stan/polling_watch_v5_epoch.stan for Fylgisvakt.

Windows, anchors, epochs and the harness

  • The window. The forecast model reads national polls from 1 Jan 2016 and kjördæmi polls from the 2021 result, 25 Sep 2021 (R/fit_polling_and_fundamentals_kjordaemi_model.R); Fylgisvakt reads from 1 Jan 2016 (R/fit_polling_watch.R), and so does the harness (WINDOW_START in R/harness/constants.R). The first poll in the window is dated 16 Jan 2016.
  • Anchor. An official result inside the window: it pins the walk (chapter 1.1). The 2024 forecast's anchors are the 2016, 2017 and 2021 results.
  • Epoch. In Fylgisvakt v5, a stretch between results that holds polls: E1 to E5, from E1 before the 2016 result to E5 after the 2024 result (chapter 3.1).
  • Cell $(E, F)$. A harness forecast of election $E$ made on date $F$. TARGETS holds ten: f180, f47, f14 and eve (180, 47, 14 and 1 days before $E$) for 2021 and 2024, and f14 and eve for 2017, the held-out confirmatory target.
  • Arm. A model the harness runs on its cells (R/harness/arms.R). There is one so far, r0, from Stan/polling_and_fundamentals_kjordaemi_r0.stan.
  • Tier 0. A check of an arm's sampler on two cells before anything is scored (harness/README.md).
  • The freeze. harness/FREEZE.md and harness/FREEZE.sha256. Until both exist and are committed, the checksum list covers FREEZE.md and every listed checksum matches, score_cell() scores no 2017 cell against its result; a 2021 or 2024 cell is scored with no freeze, so versions can be compared during development (harness/README.md).
  • The snapshot. The harness's frozen, checksummed copy of its inputs, git-ignored under harness/snapshot/ and checked against harness/snapshot_manifest.csv.
  • v2. The next forecast model, on a branch not yet merged (chapter 3.1; 4.2, planned).

What is open

Open
  • Coverage. The page holds the symbols of 1.1, 1.4 and 3.1 only. Each planned chapter adds its rows when it is written.
  • Order set by the data. Houses after the first one or two are numbered by the order the readers meet them in the data files, not by a fixed list, so the indices can change when a file is rewritten. Each reader fixes a party order, but prepare_polling_watch_data() drops a party no poll in its window reports, and a party missing from Fylgisvakt's list is placed just before Annað.
  • Same names, different objects. mu_gamma, sigma_gamma, gamma, beta and L_Omega differ in meaning or shape between the forecast model and Fylgisvakt (section Clashes).
