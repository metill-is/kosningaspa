Kosningaspá · Methodology notes

How the models work, written up

Living notes on the kosningaspá forecast model, the Fylgisvakt polling smoother and the v2 work in progress: what each model assumes, how the code implements it, and what the records say about why. Kept up to date as the models change, so that the author can stay current with them. 5 of 14 chapters written.

How to read these pages. Every chapter is drafted with Claude from the code and the project's records and is not reviewed by the author unless its stamp says so. Each page's stamp gives the date it describes (kosningaspa master on that date); unmerged work sits in purple branch boxes, recorded numbers are given with their source, and decisions are the author's, dated. The live state is kosningaspa master.

I · The forecast model

  • 1.1
    The latent support walk and its election anchors
    The forecast model's latent state: centred log-ratio levels on the poll dates, a correlated random walk run backwards from election day, faster in the 47 days before an election, pinned by each past result with Brownian bridges between results; how parties that do not exist yet are handled; and how the walk is sampled.
    Not reviewed by the author. Drafted with Claude on 4 Nov 2026 from the code on master.
    ●
  • 1.2
    Fundamentals and their weight
    planned

  • 1.3
    Constituency effects
    How the forecast model goes from national support to the six kjördæmi: one static log-ratio effect per party and kjördæmi, the sixth closed by a weighted sum whose weights change from poll to poll; the effects' scale and how they are sampled; the kjördæmi data the fit reads; the kjördæmi forecast that the seat step uses; and PR #15's kjördæmi fixes.
    Not reviewed by the author. Drafted with Claude on 4 Nov 2026 from the code on master and the Stan data the fit script builds.
    ●
  • 1.4
    From vote shares to seats
    How kosningaspá turns kjördæmi votes into Alþingi's 63 seats: D'Hondt in each kjördæmi, nine adjustment seats by national D'Hondt over the lists with at least 5%, each placed where the list's next quotient, as a share of the kjördæmi's valid votes, is largest; the two layouts; the 2021 count traced step by step; what folding lists into Annað does; and how the allocator is tested against every official seat since 2016.
    Not reviewed by the author. Drafted with Claude on 4 Nov 2026 from the code on master and the official results committed under harness/truth/.
