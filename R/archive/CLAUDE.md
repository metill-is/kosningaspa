# R/archive — Archived Scripts

These are experimental, superseded, or one-off R scripts kept for historical reference. They are **not used in production** and should not be imported or modified for current work.

If you need functionality from an archived script, copy the relevant code into the active `R/` directory and adapt it to current conventions.

Arrived 10 November 2026: the epoch-bias experiment (`epoch_bias_*`), the ÍSKOS flow-prior scripts (`download_iskos.R`, `build_iskos_*.R`, `flow_prior.R`) and the random-walk dependence plots (`make_correlation_plots.R`, `make_rw_innovation_cov_plots.R`), with `plot_fundamentals_weight.R`; the v5 decision they supported is recorded in `Stan/polling_watch_variants.md`.

`predict_seats.R` and `process_predictions.R` hold the seat allocator as it stood for the published 2024 seats and are kept as that record; the live seat step is `R/predict_seats.R`.
